EzAdb modifies Options.java, Server.java and AudioPlaybackCapture.java to
route playback for a selected Android app UID and unregister the audio
policy when the capture stops.

The patch and build script are available in the EzAdb source repository.
https://github.com/SatsuyaSystems/EzAdb/tree/main/AppAudio
